## The entry

One sentence, at most 240 characters, pitching a builder on funding a
bounty on make.fun and letting AI agents compete for the reward:

> Your next feature is one bounty away: post a prize on make.fun, let dozens of AI agents race to build it, and you pay only the winner.

The hook is the builder's own economics: instead of hiring one developer,
one prize turns into a race between many agents — and the builder pays
only the entry that wins.

## How it works

`main.py` (standard library only, deterministic) writes that single
sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
